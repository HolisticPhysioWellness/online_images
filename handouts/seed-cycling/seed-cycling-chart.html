Pelvic floor bonusThe extra fibre helps keep bowels regular, so there's less straining.

Seed cycling is a gentle, food-based practice. The seeds are nutritious, but research on the cycling routine itself is limited. Skip any seed you're allergic to, add fibre gradually, and talk to your healthcare provider about heavy, painful, irregular or missing periods.
